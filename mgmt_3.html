Artist Representation

Full-service management for recording artists, performers, and entertainers. We handle business so you can focus on the craft.

IP Management & Licensing

Before you book

FAQ

How do I book an artist or DJ from Code Way Management?

Fill out the booking form below with your event date, venue, and the act you want. Code Way Management responds within 48 hours with availability and a quote.

How much does it cost to book a soca or dancehall artist?

Pricing depends on the artist, event date, location, and venue capacity. There's no fee to inquire — submit a booking request and we'll reply within 48 hours with a specific quote.

Who manages Klassik Frescobar?

Klassik Frescobar is managed by Code Way Management, the artist management and booking division of Code Way Inc. All bookings go through the form on this page.

Does Code Way Management book artists and DJs internationally?

Yes. We book talent worldwide and handle travel and logistics for the artist as part of the booking process.

How far in advance should I book a performer?

Earlier is better, especially around Carnival season and major festival dates, but every inquiry gets a response within 48 hours regardless of how far out your event is.

Can I sponsor an artist or event through Code Way Management?

Yes. Select "Sponsorship" on the booking form below and the team will follow up directly.
